The Artist

Face Of Monrovia is a rising West African artist blending Afrobeat, Afro-fusion, and soulful storytelling. Rooted in Liberian culture and currently based in Accra, Ghana, she creates music that connects identity, ambition, love, and culture. Bold. Soulful. Stylish. Global.
