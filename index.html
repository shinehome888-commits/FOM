The Artist

▼

Face Of Monrovia (FOM) is Liberian born and raised in Liberia, now based in Accra, Ghana for studies. She is a rising West African artist blending Afrobeat, Afro-fusion, and soulful storytelling. Rooted in Liberian culture and inspired by Ghana's vibrant creative scene, she creates music that connects identity, ambition, love, and culture. Bold. Soulful. Stylish. Global.
